FAQ

What else do you need to know?

How does publishing work?

Draft and preview on your Mac, then publish to a static site on your configured host. Early access includes a default hosting setup with custom domain mapping. Prefer DIY? Export the static bundle and host it anywhere.

What’s the refund policy?

If we can’t help you create, edit, and publish a working site within 30 days of your report, we’ll refund your purchase and remove you from the founder program. Otherwise, early access is a one-time license.

Do I need the Oath or Kul?

No. VibeMaker Studio stands on its own. The Path, Oath, and Kul are optional identity layers for makers who want the philosophy and community on top of the tool.

What Mac do I need?

macOS Monterey (12.0) or newer. Apple Silicon recommended. Internet access required for publishing and updates.
