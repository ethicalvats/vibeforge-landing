FAQ

What else do you need to know?

How does publishing work?

Draft and preview on your Mac, then publish to a static site on a configured host. Early access includes a default hosting setup with custom domain mapping. Prefer DIY? Export the static bundle and host it anywhere you like.

Am I locked in?

No. VibeMaker Studio outputs standard HTML, CSS, and JSON. You can export your site, move to Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare, or your own infra, and your pages keep working even if you stop using the app.

What’s the refund policy?

If we can’t help you create, edit, and publish a working site within 30 days of your report, we’ll refund your purchase and remove you from the founder program. Otherwise, early access is a one-time license.

What Mac do I need?

macOS Monterey (12.0) or newer. Apple Silicon is recommended for the smoothest previews. Internet access is required for publishing and updates.
